# Chocolate Mug Cake

## Ingredients
- 4 tablespoons all purpose flour  
- 2 tablespoons cocoa powder  
- 2 tablespoons butter  
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder  
- Salt  
- 3 tablespoons milk  
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil  
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract  
- 2 tablespoons choclate chips   

## Instructions
1. In a microwave safe mug, mix flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt.
2. Add milk, oil, vanilla extract and stir until smooth with no lumps.
3. Mix in chocolate chips.
4. Microwave on high for **60 to 90 seconds** until the cake rises and sets.
5. *Let it cool before eating.*

---

## Notes
- **Microwave times vary**, so start at 60 seconds and add time if needed.
- Best eaten **right after it cools**.
